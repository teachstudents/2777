🍳 Grammar Lab: Recipes & Quantities

"Need" vs. "Need to"

Rule 1: "Need" + Noun

Use for physical ingredients or tools we require.

Rule 2: "Need to" + Verb

Use for cooking steps or actions we have to do.

Checking Ingredients

✅

"Enough"

The amount is just right.

Countable: "We have enough eggs."

❌

"Not enough"

Too little; must buy more.

Uncountable: "There is not enough beef."

🌟

"Plenty of"

A large quantity; more than we need.

Countable: "There are plenty of bananas."
