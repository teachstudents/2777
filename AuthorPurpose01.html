The 6 Author's Purposes

1. To Inform 📊

The author wants to give you factual information, explain a process, or teach you something new without expressing a strong opinion.

2. To Persuade 📣

The author is trying to convince you to agree with their opinion, change your mind, or take a specific action (like buying something or voting).

3. To Entertain 🎭

The author writes for your enjoyment. They want to make you laugh, cry, or feel suspense through stories, jokes, or dramatic events.

4. To Describe 🎨

The author paints a picture with words. They use rich sensory details (sight, sound, smell, touch, taste) so you can clearly imagine a person, place, or thing.

5. To Argue ⚖️

Similar to persuading, but specifically focused on taking a firm stance on a debatable or controversial issue, often providing evidence against a counter-argument.

6. To Evaluate 📝

The author is making a judgment. They are reviewing, assessing, or critiquing the quality, value, or worth of something (like a book, movie, product, or idea).
