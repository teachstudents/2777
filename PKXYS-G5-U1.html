Grade 5 ESL Master App

Unit 1: International Cuisines 🌍🍽️

🎒
Module 1

Welcome & Activities

Kick off the unit with interactive tools: Backpack icebreaker, Find Someone Who mixer, Food Vocab Flashcards, Board Game, Rock-Paper-Scissors, Riddles, and a 3-Player Emoji Match.

🎲
Module 2

Global Food Stations

A comprehensive presentation suite featuring a Student Roulette, Global Food Chants, Sentence Practice Frames, Listening Tasks, Grammar labs, Recipe Sequencer, and Verbs Race.

👨‍🍳
Module 3

Grammar Chef

A gamified drag-and-drop sentence builder. Students race against the clock to master countable/uncountable nouns (much/many/enough), cooking verbs, and taste adjectives.

✍️
Module 4

Canteen Review Project

The final project hub. Includes a visual student grouping tool, an interactive drafting template, automated dialogue roleplay scripts, and a presentation tracker.

⚠️ Note: Interactive audio features require you to tap the screen once after launching a module.
